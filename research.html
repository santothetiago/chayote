Utilizing Artificial Intelligence to Optimize Chayote (Sechium Edule) Growth and Production

Chapter I: Introduction

According to Saade, R. L. Chayote is the Nahuatl name for Sechium edule (Jacq.) Swartz, which is widely cultivated in Latin America for its edible fruits and roots. Despite its global presence and economic importance, more research is needed on its biology, improvement, relation to wild species, and genetic conservation. On a different note, the edible parts of Sechium edule are relatively low in fibre, protein, and vitamins compared with other vegetables. This, however, does not render it irrelevant as many of these nutritional characteristics make Chayote (Sechium edule) particularly suitable for hospital diets.

Accordingly, our proposal primarily focuses on using AI algorithms to accurately detect common diseases of Chayote (Sechium edule) and provide instructions for optimal development of said plants. The study intends to improve Chayote production practices, increase productivity, inspect the Chayote plants routinely for any defects, and promote sustainable agricultural development by incorporating Artificial Intelligence to encourage the usage of Chayote (Sechium edule)

Conceptual Framework

For the purpose of this study, we aim to develop an AI system based on Transfer Learning. Which is basically training a deep network from the ground up is computationally intensive and requires vast datasets, making it impractical given the project's constraints. Transfer learning leverages a pre-trained model, dramatically reducing the data and computational power required while often yielding higher accuracy.

Research Questions

  • What identifiable gaps exist in current global research regarding the application of Artificial Intelligence (specifically Transfer Learning) in identifying and optimizing the cultivation of Chayote (Sechium edule)?
  • In what ways can real-time plant health monitoring and species-specific cultivation instructions, powered by AI, outperform conventional farming methods in enhancing Chayote yield and sustainability?
  • How are problems like insect infestations, diseases, and unfavorable environmental circumstances intended to be addressed by the suggested AI technology integration in Chayote production and management practices?
  • How does Artificial Intelligence track the plant's health and know what disease it has?
  • How can Artificial Intelligence, specifically through a Transfer Learning model, be utilized to accurately detect common diseases in Chayote (Sechium edule) to enable more effective and sustainable cultivation practices?
